Problem Statement

Urban Mobility & Safety Gaps in Karachi

Navigating Pakistan's largest metropolis presents daily mobility, transit, and security hurdles.

⚠️ Current Mobility Realities

  • Information Vacuum: Commuters routinely enter flooded zones, civic protest blocks, or crime hotspots without prior warning.
  • Fragmented Information: Incident updates are trapped in disconnected WhatsApp groups and unvetted social media posts.
  • Blind Route Planning: Mainstream routing tools prioritize travel time, remaining blind to immediate community alerts.

🎯 The Opportunity

  • Centralized Civic GIS: A single map displaying geotagged incidents across Karachi.
  • Travel Corridor Awareness: Check for community reports within a 1.5 km corridor of your travel path.
  • Structured Moderation: Distinguish between unverified tips and admin-verified incidents.

Technical Architecture

Lightweight, Framework-Free Architecture

Engineered for high performance and compatibility without client-side framework overhead.

Frontend (Client)

  • HTML5 & Custom CSS3: Dark navy & teal design system.
  • Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+): Zero React/Angular bloat, instant page loading.
  • Leaflet.js + OSM: Interactive vector map tiles.
  • Chart.js v4: Dynamic administrative charts.

Backend (REST API)

  • Node.js & Express: High-throughput REST API.
  • JWT & bcryptjs: Stateless authentication & 10-round salted password hashing.
  • Rate Limiting: IP-level protection against spam reports.
  • OSRM + Resilient Fallback: Guaranteed route calculation.

Database (PostgreSQL)

  • Relational Integrity: 5 normalized tables with foreign keys.
  • Spatial Constraints: Coordinate range boundary validation.
  • Audit Logging: `verifications` table tracks every admin decision.
  • Parameterized Queries: Complete SQL injection defense.

Flagship Feature

Route Awareness & Corridor Scanning

Empowering commuters to review active community reports along their trajectory.

How Route Calculation Works

  • Coordinate Geocoding: Select points via Karachi map clicks or text search.
  • OSRM Driving Calculation: Computes the exact driving geometry, distance, and duration.
  • Corridor Scanning: Evaluates active incidents within a 1.5 km corridor along the path using the Haversine equation.
  • Resilient Fallback: If external routing is unavailable, an estimated corridor route is displayed seamlessly.

Ethical Safeguard Principle

SafeRoute Karachi never calculates a deceptive "Safety Score" or claims that any street is guaranteed safe.

Emergency service helplines (Police 15, Rescue 1122, Edhi 115) are permanently featured across all pages to clarify that the app is an informational aid, not an emergency dispatcher.

Integrity & Governance

Admin Moderation & Audit Pipeline

Combatting fake news, hoaxes, and slander through structured verification.

1. Pending State

User reports are automatically flagged as Pending. Visible on map with caution tags.

2. Inspection

Admins examine descriptions, coordinate legitimacy, and corroborating reports on the queue.

3. Decision Logging

Reports are updated to Verified, Rejected, or Expired with logged audit notes.

Permanent Audit Trail in PostgreSQL

Every administrative action is stored in the verifications table: admin_id, decision, comments, and created_at timestamp.

Engineering Standards

Security, Privacy & Data Protection

🔒 Application Security

  • Bcrypt Hashing: Passwords stored with 10 salt rounds.
  • Role Guards: Admin APIs require valid JWT and verified admin role.
  • Rate Limiting: Endpoints capped to prevent automated spam abuse.
  • SQL Injection Shield: Parameterized queries prevent SQL injection.

👤 Privacy & Ethical Engineering

  • No PII Collection: Victim names, vehicle registration numbers, or suspect identities are barred.
  • Geofence Bounds: Submissions restricted to coordinates within Karachi (lat 24.5-25.2, lng 66.5-67.5).
  • Community Terms: Guidelines posted across all submission forms.
